Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Service Name: Korean Gratitude Meditation (명상을 위한 마음 편지)

1. Purpose of Collecting and Using Personal Information

"Korean Gratitude Meditation" (hereinafter referred to as the "Service") is a pure meditation application provided entirely anonymously without any sign-up or login procedures. The Service does not collect, store, or use any sensitive personal information that can identify an individual, such as name, phone number, or email address.

2. Information Collected and Method of Collection

The Service only collects minimal anonymous data to ensure smooth operation:

  • Collected Items: An anonymous unique identifier (UUID) assigned upon installation, meditation sound mix data, and device token (FCM Token) for push notifications.
  • Purpose of Collection: Saving and loading user-created meditation sound mixes, generating anonymous invitation links, and sending service-related notifications.
  • This data is safely stored in the cloud (Supabase) in a completely anonymous state, making it impossible to identify the specific user.

3. Provision and Entrustment to Third Parties

The Service does not provide users' personal information to external third parties, nor does it entrust the processing of personal information to other companies.

4. User Rights and How to Exercise Them

Since the Service is not login-based, a separate account deletion process is not required. If you wish to delete your saved anonymous settings data and withdraw consent for receiving notifications, simply delete the app from your smartphone (or clear app data and cache). This action will immediately destroy all local data and revoke notification permissions.
